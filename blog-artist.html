About

Above & Beyond

Above & Beyond are 3 DJs, Jono Grant, Tony McGuinness and Paavo Siljamaki who teamed up to make music in the realm of EDM, specifically, the Trance Genre in 2000. Oceanlab was an older alias the group used with a vocalist, Justine Suissa.

Anjunabeats

Anjunabeats is their Record Label. This is where they release their music as well as host smaller artists of the same genre. There is also a secondary Label called Anjunadeep which was made to present the Deep House Genre

ABGT - Above & Beyond Group Therapy

The name of their Radio show which became a Tour and has Concerts hosting the full works of the Anjunabeats Record Label and the Various Artists
